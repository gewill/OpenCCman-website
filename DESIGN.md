# Website design

Retain OpenCCman blue and Apple system typography; follow the reference site’s language routes and product/privacy/support structure. Readable quiet surfaces, no decorative animation. The one exception is the click-to-play product video in the 2.0 preview section: a poster frame until the visitor starts it, with no autoplay or looping. Text conversion specimen is labeled as an example, not an app screenshot. Desktop two-column introduction collapses to one column on mobile. Honor system dark appearance, keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and native links.
