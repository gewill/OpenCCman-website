Choose between Traditional, Taiwan and Hong Kong presets

“Traditional Chinese” is not one uniform output. Choose a preset for your readers, then review the terms that matter to them.

These steps describe the publicly available 2.0 app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Features still being tested for 2.1 are listed separately in the changelog. Changelog

The four presets

Simplified Chinese
Produces Simplified characters. It is not a complete reverse conversion of Taiwan-specific words or phrases.
Traditional · OpenCC
Uses OpenCC's standard Traditional form. This is an internal conversion standard, not a promise to match every Taiwan or Hong Kong usage.
Taiwan · Standard + Idioms
Applies Taiwan character variants and Taiwan phrase conversion as well as Simplified-to-Traditional conversion. Choose it when the intended readers use Taiwan writing conventions.
Hong Kong · Traditional
Applies Hong Kong character variants. The preset does not add a separate Hong Kong idiom option in OpenCCman.

A practical choice

  1. Know your audience. For Taiwan-facing copy start with Taiwan Standard with idioms; for Hong Kong-facing copy start with Hong Kong Traditional.
  2. Keep an original. Compare the source and result, especially for names, brands, quotations and technical terms.
  3. Use advanced controls only when needed. Other combinations appear as Custom. Change one setting at a time and reconvert so you can see its effect.
OpenCC's design principles explain why its `t`, `tw` and `hk` modes are conversion conventions rather than guarantees of a particular style guide.
