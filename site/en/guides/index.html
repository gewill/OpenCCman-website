A useful order

  1. Convert a short sample and compare the source with the result.
  2. Choose the preset for the audience: OpenCC Traditional, Taiwan or Hong Kong.
  3. Bring in a TXT document only after checking its encoding and size.
  4. On Mac, set up selection shortcuts when you want to work across apps.

These steps describe the publicly available 2.0 app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Features still being tested for 2.1 are listed separately in the changelog. Changelog
