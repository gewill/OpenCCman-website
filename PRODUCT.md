## Purpose
Independent product, privacy and support website, confirmed by owner. Three languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English.

## Evidence

Release notes label 2.1 as a candidate until it is publicly released. The 2.0 home-page claims are limited to implemented Mac global shortcuts (with Accessibility/source-app caveats), local conversion, four presets, adaptive source/result layout, and one UTF-8 TXT file up to 10 MiB. The home page makes no new price claim. The product video is rendered from the private gewill/OpenCCman-motion repository. App icon from OpenCCman asset catalog.
