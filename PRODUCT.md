# OpenCCman Website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Purpose
Independent product, privacy and support website, confirmed by owner. Three languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English.

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS generated by scripts/build_site.py, with small same-origin JavaScript enhancements (proof desk, layout demo, click-to-play video) and self-hosted OFL font subsets; Cloudflare Pages with a same-origin-only CSP. Follows the requested Pingman-website reference for routes and page set. Free pages.dev address; no Functions, database, trackers or paid add-ons.

## Evidence
Privacy wording approved by owner and published on gewill.org. OpenCCman 2.0 is publicly available on both App Store platforms; the three-language site and release notes are live at openccman.gewill.org. Release notes label 2.1 as a candidate until it is publicly released. The 2.0 home-page claims are limited to implemented Mac global shortcuts (with Accessibility/source-app caveats), local conversion, four presets, adaptive source/result layout, and one UTF-8 TXT file up to 10 MiB. The home page makes no new price claim. The product video is rendered from the private gewill/OpenCCman-motion repository. App icon from OpenCCman asset catalog. Conversion examples are real OpenCC 1.4.2 output generated by scripts/specimens.py; the app's four presets map to OpenCC t2s, s2t, s2twp and s2hk (ConversionConfiguration.swift in the app repository). Owner decisions of 2026-09-29: Traditional Chinese navigation uses 更新紀錄 and 隱私權政策; the privacy pages carry margin headings beside the unchanged policy paragraphs.
